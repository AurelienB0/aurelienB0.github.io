Privacy Policy for Pollinum Last Updated: February 11, 2026 1. INTRODUCTION Welcome to Pollinum. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data in our free mobile application. 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT We only collect data strictly necessary to provide the service: • Account Data: Email address and name (provided via Clerk). • Plant Data: Names, species, and "More Info" notes for your plants. • Care Logs: Timestamps of when you last completed a care task. 3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES We use the following services to run the App: • Authentication (Clerk): Manages secure sign-in. We do not see or store your passwords. • Database (Supabase): Stores your plant data. Access is restricted so only you can see your own data. • Notifications (Expo): Triggers local alerts on your device. 4. DATA RETENTION & DELETION • Retention: Data is kept as long as your account exists. • Deletion: You can delete individual plants at any time. You can also use the "Delete Account" button in Settings to instantly and permanently wipe all your data from our servers. 5. SECURITY We use industry-standard encryption through Clerk and Supabase. However, no internet-based service is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security. 6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be noted by updating the "Last Updated" date above. 7. CONTACT If you have questions about your privacy, contact us at: pollinum.app@gmail.com
